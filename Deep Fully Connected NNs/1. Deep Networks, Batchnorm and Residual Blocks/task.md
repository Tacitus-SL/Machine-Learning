# Deep Networks, BatchNorm, and Residual Blocks

Explore why deeper fully connected networks can be hard to train on MNIST. In `task.ipynb`, build bottleneck networks, implement batch normalization and residual blocks, and compare their gradient behavior. The notebook links to the relevant implementation files under `source/code/src/`.

The checker uses a small offline dataset. The optional experiment section lets you train the networks for longer and compare how normalization and skip connections affect learning.
